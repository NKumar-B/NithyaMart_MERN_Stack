>

>

>

> 
>4. *Visual polish* — Use canvas globalCompositeOperation blending for glowing effects. Animate particles with physics (velocity, fade, gravity). Make the release feel fast and impactful with a quick flash and ripple.
> 
>*Tech stack:* Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS + MediaPipe Hands CDN + Canvas API. Single self-contained HTML file. Access the webcam via getUserMedia. Overlay the canvas on top of the live video feed.
